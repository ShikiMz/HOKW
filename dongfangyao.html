RResonance Skill
归尘 Return to Dust
Skill Damage Recovery Stun Cooldown: 11s

When casting a movement skill, generates a Star Mark (星辰标记), recording the current position. Casting Return to Dust returns to the Star Mark location, dealing Resonance Skill damage to enemies along the path.

In Radiant Light (辉光) state, deals higher damage, recovers 14% of lost HP, and can counter enemies' Red-Light Skills.

Return to Dust Damage归尘伤害176%
Empowered Return to Dust Damage强化归尘伤害445%

In Ranked Mode, Return to Dust removes damage detection at the starting position.


In Radiant Light state, must hit an enemy to gain HP recovery, recovering 12% of lost HP.

✦Passive Mechanic
星辰之赐 Star Blessing
Skill Empowered Normal Attack

Each time a Resonance Skill is cast, gain 1 stack of Star Force (星辰之力), up to 3 stacks. When fully stacked, enter Radiant Light (辉光) state, which empowers the next Resonance Skill cast.

The Radiant Light state ends after casting the Empowered Resonance Skill, and empowers the next normal attack into Star-Shattering Strike (碎星式), dealing normal attack damage.

共流技 · Coordinated Resonance Skill
Triggered after the on-field Resonator casts their Resonance Liberation. Upon entering, quickly dashes through, dealing Resonance Skill damage to enemies within range.

Shooting Star Chain Flash
After casting Shooting Star Strike (飞星式), the 3rd skill is replaced by Return to Dust Flash (归尘闪回). Consumes 30 combo points when cast, and can be used up to 4 times. After casting 归尘闪回, the next normal attack is empowered into 飞星式.
Layer 10 · 第十层 — Node B
飞星如雨
Shooting Stars Like Rain
After casting the Empowered Normal Attack Star-Shattering Strike (碎星式), long-pressing Normal Attack again can release Shooting Star Strike (飞星式). Hitting with 碎星式 or 飞星式 grants 1% Resonance Liberation energy.
Layer 12 · 第十二层
浩荡星河
Vast Flowing Galaxy
When Star-Shattering Strike (碎星式) or Shooting Star Strike (飞星式) hits enemies in the Star Erosion (星削) state, gain a 4% damage increase lasting 12 seconds, up to a maximum of 32%.

After being offered by the Dragon Princess of the undersea Dragon Palace to the Eagle Yang Cavalry heading north, it disappeared from historical records.
