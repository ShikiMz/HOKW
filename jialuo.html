LMBBasic Attack
普通攻击 Normal Attack
Skill Damage Stun

Continuously shoot, dealing normal attack damage.

After a Perfect Dodge or casting Skill 2 Silent Arrow (静默之箭), briefly gain Flash Consecutive Shot (瞬华连射) effect, empowering the next normal attack. In Short Bow State (短弓状态), the next normal attack rapidly fires four arrows — on hit, gain 2 Soul Ferry Arrows (渡灵箭) and can counter enemies' Red-Light Skills. In Long Bow State (长弓状态), the next normal attack additionally fires one Demon-Chasing Arrow (追魔箭) and can counter enemies' Red-Light Skills.

蓄力普攻 · Charged Normal Attack
Pull the bowstring fully to enter Precision Shot (精准射击) state. Charge lasts up to 1 second; the fierce arrow fired pierces through enemies.
In Ranked Mode, only Charged Attacks cause suppression; all other normal attacks do not.
QResonance Skill
渡灵之箭 Soul Ferry Arrow
Skill Switch

Enter Long Bow State (长弓状态), empowering normal attacks to Soul Ferry Shot (渡灵射击). Casting again or when Soul Ferry Arrows (渡灵箭) are exhausted, switch back to Short Bow State (短弓状态).

渡灵射击 · Soul Ferry Shot
Fire Soul Ferry Arrows (渡灵箭), dealing normal attack damage on hit. Continuously casting Soul Ferry Shot gradually increases attack speed. While inside Pure Domain (纯净领域), arrows fly like the wind — the attack speed increase effect is doubled.

On Soul Ferry Shot critical hit, additionally fire one Demon-Chasing Arrow (追魔箭), dealing normal attack damage.

EResonance Skill
静默之箭 Silent Arrow
Skill Control Stun Cooldown: 20s

Draw the bow and fire a piercing arrow in the specified direction, dealing Resonance Skill damage, knocking back enemies and leaving a rune. After a brief delay, the rune explodes, dealing Resonance Skill damage to nearby enemies, launching them airborne, and countering enemies' Red-Light Skills.

After casting, gain 2 Soul Ferry Arrows (渡灵箭); the next normal attack within 3 seconds is empowered to Flash Consecutive Shot (瞬华连射).

RResonance Skill
纯净之域 Pure Domain
Skill Buff Cooldown: 30s

Release arcane power to deploy a Pure Domain (纯净领域) lasting 12 seconds, immediately launching airborne all enemies within the domain. While self is within the domain, Crit Rate is increased. Casting again closes the Pure Domain.

TResonance Liberation
千窟神箭 Thousand Cavern Divine Arrow
Skill Burst Stun Cooldown: 15s

Condense demon-breaking power into the Thousand Cavern Divine Arrow (千窟神箭), and fire it fully drawn in the specified direction, dealing massive Resonance Liberation damage to all enemies within range. Enemies hit are stunned from the powerful impact. Can counter enemies' Red-Light Skills.

In Ranked Mode, the untargetable effect is changed to reducing all damage received by 50%.
✦Passive Mechanic
箭雨风息 Arrow Rain Wind Breath
Skill Shield Counter

All attacks deal 100% additional damage to shields.

In Short Bow State (短弓状态), gain 1 Soul Ferry Arrow (渡灵箭) every 3 seconds, up to a maximum of 10.

Using Skill 1 Soul Ferry Arrow (渡灵之箭) switches between Short Bow State and Long Bow State.

共流技 · Coordinated Resonance Skill
Triggered after the on-field Resonator casts their Resonance Liberation. Upon entering, fire up to 10 arrows at enemies, dealing Resonance Skill damage.

In Ranked Mode, Soul Ferry Arrow (渡灵箭) default cap is changed to 5.


Flash Consecutive Shot (瞬华连射) hits no longer grant Soul Ferry Arrows.
