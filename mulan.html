E Resonance Skill II
旋舞之华 Spinning Petal
Skill Resource Cooldown: 12s

Throw a dual blade forward; on hit it attaches to the enemy and orbits them, dealing Attribute Skill DMG once every 0.5s for 6s. Re-activating instantly teleports to the blade's location, picks it up, and delivers a powerful slash dealing Attribute Skill DMG. On hit, grants Fierce Blade Intent (迅烈剑意).

Q Resonance Skill I
苍破斩 Azure Cleave
Skill Resource Major Stun

Launch a Charge (冲撞) forward, Parrying (化解) enemy attacks during the dash; then follow up with a sword slash dealing Attribute Skill DMG. Mulan gains 50% damage reduction while this skill is active. Long-press to charge the skill — Mulan enters Armored (霸体) state during the charge. If charge time exceeds 3.3s, 苍破斩 upgrades into 苍破强袭.

Azure Surge · 苍破强袭
Mulan enters an Invincible state and deals Attribute Skill DMG. Can counter enemy Red-Light Skills. 苍破强袭 consumes all Azure Marks (苍破印记) to call down a giant greatsword from above, dealing Attribute Skill DMG as a follow-up pursuit strike.

✦ Skill Break / Passive
长城巡守者 Great Wall Guardian
Skill Switch Buff

In Dual Blades Form (轻剑形态): landing normal attacks and Attribute Skills on enemies grants Fierce Blade Intent (迅烈剑意).

In Greatsword Form (重剑形态): using E skill 迅烈之华 consumes Fierce Blade Intent to deal bonus damage and gain Azure Mark (苍破印记) stacks.

Using R skill 绕放刀锋 switches between 轻剑形态 and 重剑形态.

Co-op Resonance · 共流技
After an ally uses their Ultimate on-field, slash at nearby enemies with the dual blade; on hit, leap airborne and follow up with a greatsword downward slam, dealing Attribute Skill DMG.
In Ranked Mode, the first hit of the Co-op Resonance (共流技) stunning enemies for 1.5s upon landing.

绕刀剑舞
Whirling Blade Dance
When R skill 绕放刀锋 successfully Parries (化解) an attack, instantly restore 500 points of Fierce Blade Intent (迅烈剑意) and convert the next normal attack into a Perfect Dodge Counter (完美闪避反击). This effect is inactive in Ranked Mode.

剑意充盈
Brimming Blade Intent

Layer 6 · 第六层
军锋临阵
Vanguard's Edge
In Greatsword Form (重剑形态), each critical hit from E skill 迅烈之华 grants an additional 1 stack of Azure Mark (苍破印记).
Layer 6 · 第六层
不怯虎威
Fearless Before the Tiger
Successfully Parrying (化解) with R skill 绕放刀锋, or with Greatsword Form Q skill 苍破斩, grants 4 stacks of Azure Mark (苍破印记).
Layer 8 · 第八层
巡守者意志
Guardian's Will
After Ultimate 巡守者之刀 is used, gain 1000 points of Fierce Blade Intent (迅烈剑意) and make the next Greatsword Form Q skill 苍破斩 automatically fully charged as 苍破强袭.
Layer 10 · 第十层
忘我酣战
Lost in Battle
Each stack of Azure Mark (苍破印记) consumed increases Mulan's own ATK by 5%. This effect stacks up to 6 times and lasts 10s.
Layer 10 · 第十层
瀚海鏖兵
Desert Siege
When holding at least 4 stacks of Azure Mark (苍破印记), 苍破强袭 deals an additional 50% bonus damage.
Layer 12 · 第十二层
百花缭乱
Petal Storm
Every 24 stacks of Azure Mark (苍破印记) consumed automatically triggers a powerful follow-up pursuit strike against the enemy.
