追击率 · Follow-Up Attack Rate Mechanics
100% → Guarantees 1 additional Normal Attack follow-up.
150% → Guarantees 1 follow-up + 50% chance for an additional 2nd Normal Attack follow-up.
200% → Guarantees 2 follow-up Normal Attacks.

流相 · Flow Affinities
Each hero has a Flow Affinity (流相) that determines the damage type their attacks deal. Attacking enemies with a matching weakness triggers bonus damage based on Weakness Exploit (流相克制强度). The individual flow DMG bonus stat (e.g. 贯流强度) increases that damage type specifically.

细分标签 · Breakdown Labels
Tapping any stat in-game reveals a breakdown of its sources. These labels appear across all stats — here are their translations.
Base
基础
The total base value of the stat before any bonuses.
Character Base
共鸣基础
The portion of the base stat that comes from your character's own level and growth.
Weapon Base
凝武基础
The portion of the base stat contributed by your equipped 凝武 (Resonance Weapon).
Bonus
加成
The total bonus added on top of the base stat from all sources.
Flow Bonus
流脉加成
Bonus from your character's Flow Vein (流脉) passive node tree.
Gear Bonus
装备加成
Bonus from equipped gear and accessories.
Inscription Bonus
铭文加成
Bonus from Inscriptions (铭文) applied to your equipment.
Other Bonuses
其他加成
Miscellaneous bonuses from buffs, temporary effects, or other sources.
