EResonance Skill II
助推跳跃Boost Jump
Skill Resource Damage Stun Cooldown: 12s

After a brief wind-up, controls mechanical wings to jet-propel a high leap toward a designated location, gaining Frenzy Gauge (亢奋值). On landing, deploys a force field dealing Flow Skill DMG to nearby enemies and countering their Red-Light Skills (红光技). For 8 seconds afterward, enters Rotation On-Field (轮番上阵) state — own Normal ATK is enhanced to Mechanical Wing Combo (机械翼连打), and No. 5 (鲁班五号) continuously fires long-range shockwaves dealing Flow Skill DMG.

Re-cast: controls mechanical wings to dash toward enemies and attack. Uses force field to capture No. 5 and hurl it at enemies, dealing Flow Skill DMG and gaining Frenzy Gauge (亢奋值); then exits Rotation On-Field state.

机械翼连打 · Mechanical Wing Combo
Controls mechanical wings, activates energy to form light-blade slashes against enemies in up to 3 hits. On hit, deals Normal ATK DMG and gains No. 8 Energy (八号能量).

RResonance Skill III
电能链接Electric Energy Link
Skill Buff Damage Requires max 亢奋值

Can only be cast when Frenzy Gauge (亢奋值) is at maximum. Immediately triggers Skill I Energy Shockwave (蓄能震击), then No. 5 (鲁班五号) enters Frenzy State (亢奋状态), activating electric energy to link them together. During this time, gain a 30% Crit DMG increase. No. 5 in Frenzy State continuously uses Hurricane Assault (飓风突袭).

TResonance Liberation
稷下科技Jixia Technology
Skill Buff

Casting any Flow Skill summons No. 5 (鲁班五号) for automatic combat, dealing Flow Skill DMG to enemies. No. 5 disappears when self goes to the backline or exits combat.

No. 5 dealing DMG grants a certain amount of Frenzy Gauge (亢奋值). When Frenzy Gauge reaches maximum, casting Skill III Electric Energy Link (电能链接) causes No. 5 to enter Frenzy State (亢奋状态), which persists even when self is in the backline.

共流技 · Resonance Skill
Triggered when a front-line resonance partner casts their Ultimate. On entering battle, uses mechanical wings to launch multiple attacks on the target, then summons No. 5 (鲁班五号) to plunge-attack from above, dealing Flow Skill DMG.

✦Skill Break
机械指令Mechanical Command
Skill Damage Transform Stun Cooldown: 15s

When No. 8 Energy (八号能量) is not full: uses mechanical wings to attack enemies multiple times dealing Flow Skill DMG, then summons No. 5 (鲁班五号) to plunge from above, dealing Flow Skill DMG and gaining Frenzy Gauge (亢奋值).

When No. 8 Energy is full: casts Descent Command (降临指令).

降临指令 · Descent Command
Summons and pilots No. 8 (鲁班八号), gaining a shield lasting 12 seconds — when it expires, the pilot state ends. On landing, deals Ultimate DMG to surrounding enemies and counters their Red-Light Skills (红光技). While piloting, special Normal Attacks dealing Ultimate DMG can be used; Flow Skills cannot be used during this period. No. 8's first 5 Normal ATK hits also count as Normal ATK DMG; the landing hit and the final Normal ATK hit also count as Flow Skill DMG.
